INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
All observed manipulation_type categories are associated with a single target label.
Therefore, manipulation_type behaves as a deterministic proxy for the observed label in the current dataset.
This finding requires investigation of the original label-generation procedure.

A deterministic relationship alone does not establish target leakage.
Target leakage should be assessed by examining how the target label was originally constructed and whether manipulation_type represents information that would be unavailable at real-world verification time.
